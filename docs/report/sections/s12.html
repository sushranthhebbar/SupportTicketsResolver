Appendix D. Workbooks and reproduction

Submitted with this report. The five stage workbooks (discovery, requirements version 2.0, prompt library, sprint plan, revision log) and the effort log in the workbooks folder, and the full repository in the source code folder. Supporting documents in the repository: docs/governance.md, docs/governance_coverage_check.md, docs/validation_draft_review.md, docs/validation_latency_and_fairness.md, docs/development_error_analysis.md, docs/accuracy_uncertainty_and_fairness.md, docs/urgency_and_label_noise.md and docs/credential_scan.md.

Evaluation runs. First validation run, 1 November 2026: 6 of 80 tickets before rate limiting. Paced run, 2 November: 80 of 80, prompt PR-03 v2, intent 88.8%. Final run in the final configuration: complete by 10 November, 80 of 80 with no errors, intent 91.3%, report regenerated 11 November. Hidden 120-ticket set: not run by me, zero times.

Reproduction. Install the requirements, copy .env.example to .env and add a model key. Run python -m pytest tests -v for the tests. Run python scripts/run_evaluation.py --input <tickets.json> --output <results.jsonl> --processor src.pipeline:process_ticket for an evaluation, adding --resume to continue an interrupted one, and uvicorn src.app:app for the review page. The demonstrations are scripts/demo_guardrail_ticket.py and scripts/demo_failure_modes.py.

Table E2. Estimates, inferences and judgements
ItemWhat is uncertain
Hallucination and citation accuracyNot measured. As a stand-in, one reader (the AI assistant, not a person) read the 50 validation drafts against the cited articles: 39 were sound, 7 contained a small assumption or embellishment, and 4 would mislead a reviewer who only skims. That is a read, not a rate. It cannot support a claim against the 5% and 95% targets.
Cause of the slow tickets21 of 80 tickets took 42 to 62 seconds in every outcome group. I believe they waited on the provider's rate limit, but I did not log the cause, so this is an inference.
Fairness conclusionsGroups of 8 to 61 tickets cannot show a gap under about 20 points. "No evidence of a gap" is weaker than "no gap". Fairness of draft correctness and tone was not assessed.
Does the prompt gain generalise?The development gain came from roughly nine scenarios. On validation the two prompts differ by two tickets. I have no further unseen data to test another change.
Why identical texts carry different urgency labelsUnknown. The supplied context (tier, fluency, channel, region) does not explain it. It may reflect customer circumstances that the text does not show.
Free-tier modelI do not know whether Qwen 3.8 27B on Groq's free tier behaves like the model CloudServe would run.
Risk ratingsThe likelihood and impact ratings in Table 7 are my own judgement, not measurements.
Effort logHours and daily entries are estimates reconstructed on 11 November from commit dates and project notes (about 80 hours in total to that date). Some task dates are approximate.
Agent behaviourWhether agents would catch a misleading draft is untested, because there was no pilot.
